\subsection{Oracle decomposition}

\input{tables/tab_oracle}

Because the answer is recomputed by an interpreter from an explicit expression tree, the pipeline can
be cut at each stage and fed perfect information:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Evidence & Plan & What the gap tells you \\
\midrule
predicted & predicted & the real system \\
\textbf{gold} & predicted & how much error came from \emph{seeing} \\
predicted & \textbf{gold} & how much error came from \emph{reasoning} \\
gold & gold & the interpreter's own ceiling \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

This is the most informative analysis available here, and it is only possible because the arithmetic
was moved out of the model. A system that answers directly cannot be cut this way: every error is
simply ``wrong''.

\subsection{Does synthetic plan supervision transfer?}

Typed-plan supervision is drawn predominantly from synthetic charts by necessity, since only about
$5.7\%$ of real questions yield a unique plan. Whether that transfers is the hypothesis the design
rests on, and it is measured directly: plan validity and interpreter agreement on real charts versus
synthetic ones.
